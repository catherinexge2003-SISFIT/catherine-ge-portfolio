Selected Independent Research Projects

HINTS 7 Secondary Analysis

| 2026 · Complex survey analysis
  • Secondary analysis of the U.S. National Cancer Institute HINTS 7 (2024) public-use dataset.
  • Survey-weighted logistic regression using the full-sample weight and 50 JK1 replicate weights; ordinal and model-specification sensitivity analyses.
  • Primary adjusted association: OR 1.83 (95% CI 1.42-2.34; n=5,183). Cross-sectional, non-causal interpretation.

Reproducible project

HINTS 6 + HINTS 7 Repeated Cross-Sectional Analysis

| 2026 · Survey-year interaction
  • Pooled analysis of independent 2022 and 2024 HINTS cycles using the NCI Rizzo merging design with 100 replicate weights.
  • Primary survey-year interaction: OR 1.20 (95% CI 0.83-1.73; pooled n=10,838; p=0.330).
  • The descriptive gap was larger in 2024, but the formal interaction did not establish a change in the focal association.

Reproducible project

NHANES Device-Measured Movement Analysis

| 2026 · Device-linked behavioural data / complex survey
  • NHANES 2013-2014 secondary analysis integrating day-level wrist physical-activity-monitor summaries, GPAQ leisure activity, demographics and BMI.
  • Primary model used MEC weights, masked strata and PSUs. Device outcome is MIMS movement density, not MVPA minutes.
  • Higher self-reported leisure activity was positively associated with device-measured movement density: beta 0.00876 (95% CI 0.00347-0.01404; n=4,726; p=0.003).
  • The focal association remained positive across five prespecified movement-data completeness rules and additional model sensitivities.
