How Practice Informs Research

Useful for generating questions; insufficient by itself for research claims.

Observe recurring problems

Identify repeated questions about comprehension, trust, engagement and sustained action.

Describe the context

Document platform environments, content formats and audience-facing communication patterns.

Move to governed research

Turn practice observations into explicit research questions, methods and governed data collection or analysis.

Data Boundary

Practice analytics are not a pre-existing research cohort.

Audience counts, interaction metrics and archived content are presented as historical practice records. Any future research using platform or participant data would require an appropriate research design, governance, ethics and data-management pathway.
