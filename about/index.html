About SISFIT Academy

From professional health education to researchable questions.

SISFIT developed from exercise and health-education practice into a large-scale digital practice environment that now informs research questions in digital health, health communication and behaviour change.

Our Journey

Practice first. Research development next.

Early Practice

Professional Education Foundations

Catherine Ge and Starr Cai developed exercise and health-education practice that later informed SISFIT Academy's public-facing work.

Founder

Catherine Ge

Prospective Doctoral Researcher · Digital Health & Behaviour Change

Catherine Ge combines a Master of Teaching with years of health-education practice. Her current research interests include digital health literacy, behaviour change, physical activity, digital health interventions, implementation science and health communication.

Through SISFIT, she has built a practice environment that generates testable questions about how people encounter, interpret and act on health information online.

Practice & Knowledge Translation

Professional education provides context, not academic-output status.

SISFIT's practitioner background helps explain where Catherine's research questions come from. Practice materials and platform analytics are presented as practice context and knowledge translation, not as peer-reviewed research outputs.

Health & Exercise Education

Exercise, rehabilitation and health-education materials for professional and public audiences.

Curriculum & Training

Translation of technical exercise-science guidance into structured curricula and practitioner education.

Public Health Communication

Long-term digital communication practice across multiple platforms, creating context for research on comprehension, engagement and behaviour.

Practice-to-Research Philosophy

Engagement and evidence are related problems, not interchangeable metrics.

SISFIT examines how health information can remain accessible and compelling without losing scientific rigor, and how those communication choices can be translated into researchable questions.
