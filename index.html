Meet Our Founder

Catherine Ge

Prospective Doctoral Researcher · Digital Health & Behaviour Change

Catherine Ge combines a Master of Teaching with years of health-education practice. Her current research interests include digital health literacy, behaviour change, physical activity, digital health interventions, implementation science and health communication.

Through SISFIT, she has built a large-scale practice environment that generates testable questions about how people encounter, interpret and act on health information online. She is now extending this work through formal research-methods training and prospective doctoral research.

Practice-to-Research Model

A practical tension: engagement and evidence.

SISFIT examines a practical tension between engagement and evidence: how can health information remain accessible and compelling without losing scientific rigor? Our platform experience provides a practice context for developing and testing that question.
