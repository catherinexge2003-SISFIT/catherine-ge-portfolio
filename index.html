Catherine Ge · Research development · Digital health

From health education to research.

How can people judge, understand and act on physical-activity advice in digital environments? Catherine Ge investigates questions emerging from health-education practice through reproducible secondary analysis, evidence synthesis and research-methods training.

Research direction

Digital health literacy and communication.

The connecting question is how content and digital contexts relate to the way people evaluate health information and translate it into physical-activity decisions. Research questions are being refined through formal methods training and engagement with potential supervisors.
