From Practice to Research

My background combines a Master of Teaching with years of health-education, curriculum and digital-content practice.

As the founder of SISFIT, I reached a practical limit: platform metrics could show which content attracted attention, but not whether people understood the information or changed health behaviour. That gap now defines my research direction.

I am developing the methodological foundations to study this problem rigorously through systematic evidence synthesis, formal research-design training, and prospective observational, qualitative, mixed-methods or pilot intervention work under appropriate institutional supervision and governance.
