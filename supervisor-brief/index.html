Research direction

How do digital health literacy, content design and platform environments shape whether people can judge, understand and act on physical-activity guidance? The final doctoral question is intended to be refined with the supervisory and project context.

Why this line of work

Large-scale health-education practice showed a persistent gap between attention and evidence of understanding or behaviour change. Catherine's current work moves from that practice problem toward reproducible analysis, evidence synthesis and formal research design.
