Featured Case · HINTS

Health Information Judgement & Decision Use

HINTS 7: adjusted OR 1.83 (95% CI 1.42–2.34; n=5,183). The HINTS 6+7 interaction does not establish a change across cycles (OR 1.20, p=0.330).

Research insight: association within one cycle and difference between cycles are distinct statistical questions.

Measurement · NHANES

Device-Measured Movement vs Self-Report

Device MIMS movement density and reported leisure-time activity represent related yet non-identical constructs (n=4,726).

Method insight: survey weighting, sensitivity to valid-day rules and why neither measure can simply be treated as a gold standard.
