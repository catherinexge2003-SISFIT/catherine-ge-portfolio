## Email

Dear Professor Fukuoka,

We are Catherine Ge and Starr Choi, maintaining a small open research micro-map of 10 digital physical-activity intervention studies. We included your 2019 mPED randomized clinical trial as PA010.

Before carrying the coding into a later version, could you check one specific point about the maintenance phase?

Our current coding describes months 3–9 as **continued mPED app + accelerometer** in the plus group versus **accelerometer only** in the regular group, and labels the added app mechanism as self-monitoring/feedback.

Our reading of the paper is that, during months 3–9, the plus group was specifically encouraged to continue using the physical-activity diary, while the daily messages/video clips are described for the initial 3-month intervention. Would it therefore be more accurate to code the added maintenance component more narrowly as **continued app diary/self-monitoring (+ accelerometer)**, rather than as continuation of the full acquisition-phase app package?

If that distinction is correct, we will update the next version and record the correction transparently. If our current wording is already accurate, we will retain it.
