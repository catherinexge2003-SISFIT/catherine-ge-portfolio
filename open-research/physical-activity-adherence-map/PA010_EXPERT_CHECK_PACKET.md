# PA010 Expert Check Packet

## Purpose

A one-page expert check for **PA010** in *Physical Activity Adherence — Digital Intervention Micro Map v0.1*.

This is not a claim that the source paper is wrong. The purpose is to check whether our **maintenance-phase coding** is precise enough for a later version.

## Source study

Fukuoka Y, Haskell W, Lin F, Vittinghoff E. (2019). *Short- and Long-term Effects of a Mobile Phone App in Conjunction With Brief In-Person Counseling on Physical Activity Among Physically Inactive Women: The mPED Randomized Clinical Trial.* JAMA Network Open, 2(5), e194281.

Study DOI: https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2019.4281

## Current v0.1 coding

- **Study ID:** PA010
- **Acquisition phase:** brief in-person counseling + mPED app + accelerometer
- **Maintenance phase:** continued app + accelerometer in the plus group vs accelerometer only in the regular group
- **Mechanism label:** goal setting; self-monitoring; feedback; graded goals; barrier problem solving; social support; relapse prevention; prompts/reminders; education

## Specific ambiguity

Our reading is that during months 3–9, the plus group was specifically encouraged to continue using the **physical-activity diary**, whereas daily messages/video clips are described for the initial 3-month intervention.

That raises a coding question:

> Should the added maintenance component be coded more narrowly as **continued app diary / self-monitoring (+ accelerometer)** rather than as continuation of the full acquisition-phase app package?

## Proposed correction for v0.2, if confirmed

**Current wording**

> App + counseling acquisition phase; app continuation for maintenance

**More precise maintenance wording**

> App + counseling acquisition phase; continued app diary/self-monitoring + accelerometer during maintenance

This would preserve the study result while narrowing the mechanism attribution.

## What counts as a useful expert response

Any one of the following is sufficient:

1. Confirm the narrower maintenance coding.
2. Confirm the current coding is already accurate.
3. Provide a more precise alternative wording.
