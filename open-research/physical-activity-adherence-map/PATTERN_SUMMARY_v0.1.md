# Pattern Summary — Micro Map v0.1

## Scope and limit
This is a **descriptive micro-map of 10 selected adult digital physical-activity intervention studies**, not a systematic review or meta-analysis. Patterns below describe this v0.1 set only.

## 1. Self-monitoring + feedback is the common backbone
Across all 10 coded studies, some form of self-monitoring, behavioral feedback, activity display, diary, or monitoring-supported feedback appears in the intervention structure.

`observe behavior → compare with a reference/goal → receive feedback/prompt → act again`

## 2. Adding more digital contact does not reliably add more value
Extra SMS, social comparison, or prolonged app exposure did not consistently add durable benefit.

## 3. Short-term response and maintenance are different problems
Several interventions show early differences that diminish or disappear later.

## 4. Relative advantage can conceal absolute decline or maintenance
Every future row should preserve:
`baseline direction + between-group contrast + time point`

## 5. Behavior outcomes and physiological outcomes must remain separate
`intervention exposure → behavior → sustained dose → physiological adaptation`

## 6. Multi-component packages create an attribution problem
Package-level effects often cannot identify which component caused the change.

## Candidate question for v0.2
**Under what conditions does a digital self-monitoring + feedback loop remain behaviorally salient long enough to support physical-activity maintenance?**
