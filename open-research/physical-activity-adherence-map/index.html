Physical Activity Adherence — Digital Intervention Micro Map v0.1

身体活动依从性 — 数字干预微地图 v0.1

A descriptive micro-map of 10 selected adult digital physical-activity intervention studies, structured around intervention components, behaviour-change mechanisms, time points and maintenance.

This project is jointly maintained by Catherine Ge and Starr Choi. It sits within SISFIT Open Research because it overlaps directly with Catherine's research interests in behaviour change, physical activity and digital health, while remaining methodologically distinct from her independent secondary-analysis projects.

Working Model

From measurement to repeated action

measurement → personal feedback → goal / reference → prompt / support → repeat

The v0.1 map suggests that the more useful design question is not simply whether an intervention includes tracking, but what happens after measurement and whether the resulting feedback loop stays behaviorally salient during maintenance.

Patterns Visible in v0.1

Six recurring design issues

Self-monitoring + feedback recur

Tracking technologies vary, but monitoring-linked feedback is a common backbone across this set.

More contact is not reliably more value

Extra SMS, social comparison or prolonged app exposure did not consistently add durable benefit.

Acquisition ≠ maintenance

Several early between-group differences diminished or disappeared at later time points.

Relative advantage ≠ absolute increase

A group can look better because it declines less from baseline.

Behaviour ≠ physiological capacity

More walking or leisure-time activity does not automatically imply improved fitness outcomes.

Packages create attribution problems

Multi-component trials often cannot identify which component actually produced the observed difference.

Method Boundary

What v0.1 does and does not claim

This is a convenience micro-map for structural comparison and correction. It is not a systematic review, meta-analysis or intervention-effectiveness ranking. Mechanism labels remain descriptive; BCTTv1 numeric codes are not inferred; null findings are retained; and time point, absolute direction and between-group contrast are kept distinct.

Candidate v0.2 question: Under what conditions does a digital self-monitoring + feedback loop remain behaviorally salient long enough to support physical-activity maintenance?
