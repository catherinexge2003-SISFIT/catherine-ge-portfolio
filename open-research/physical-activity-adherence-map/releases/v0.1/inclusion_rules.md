# Inclusion Rules — Micro Map v0.1

## Include
1. Adult participants (18+).
2. Target is physical activity or exercise behavior.
3. Intervention contains a digital component (e.g., app, SMS, wearable-linked feedback, web intervention, digital coaching).
4. Study reports an observable behavioral outcome (e.g., steps, MVPA, exercise-session completion).
5. A peer-reviewed paper and DOI or equivalent stable public record are available.

## Temporarily exclude
- Drug-only studies.
- Fully offline interventions with no digital component.
- Studies reporting knowledge/attitude only, with no behavioral outcome.
- Reviews/commentaries without a specific intervention to code.
- Studies where core intervention-method information cannot be established from accessible sources.

## v0.1 scope rule
This is a structural map, not a meta-analysis. Inclusion does not imply evidence quality, clinical effectiveness, or superiority.
