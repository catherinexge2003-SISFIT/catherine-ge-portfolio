## v0.2 research direction

After self-monitoring and feedback became near-default components of digital physical-activity interventions, what determines whether those mechanisms remain behaviorally salient long enough to support maintenance?

## Core refresh additions

- PA011 — Simoes et al. (2026): response-adaptive SMART intervention
- PA012 — Sagelv et al. / ONWARDS (2025): long-term nested digital components
- PA013 — Vos et al. / SNapp (2025): JITAI + perceived-usefulness moderation
- PA014 — Brickwood et al. (2021): post-program maintenance + digital versus human support
- PA015 — Yoshimura et al. (2022): long-duration app exposure + behavior/weight divergence
