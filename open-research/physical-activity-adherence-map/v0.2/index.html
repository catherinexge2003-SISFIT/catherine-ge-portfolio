Working Model

From digital support to durable incremental value

support available → support noticed → support relevant → support actionable → action repeated → behaviour preserved

This sequence is a hypothesis-generating bridge, not a validated causal model. v0.2 asks what the digital layer adds beyond an already active self-monitoring or feedback backbone, whether participants meaningfully receive that added support, and how the result changes across acquisition, continuous treatment and maintenance phases.

What v0.2 Adds

Five structural fields beyond v0.1

Phase structure

Separates acquisition, continuous active treatment, explicit maintenance and post-intervention follow-up.

Shared backbone / active comparator

Shows what both groups already receive before attributing value to the incremental digital component.

Adaptation / personalization

Distinguishes fixed packages, static tailoring, dynamic goal adaptation, JITAI and response-adaptive sequencing.

Engagement / exposure

Records evidence that support was actually used, read, worn or otherwise received; missingness alone is not treated as proof of disengagement.

Maintenance interpretation

Separates sustained gain, preserved behaviour, attenuation, no maintenance advantage and designs that do not actually test maintenance.

Incremental effect language

Null findings are described at the level supported by the study. “No incremental behavioral effect despite documented exposure” is preferred over unmeasured claims that a mechanism failed.

Cross-Era Structural Reading

Current working patterns

Self-monitoring remains the backbone

The more informative contrast is increasingly what is added around monitoring and feedback, not whether monitoring exists at all.

Innovation moved toward adaptation

Recent rows include contextual JITAI and response-adaptive SMART designs, while earlier rows more often vary fixed components or static tailoring.

Active comparators reduce incremental headroom

When both groups already receive substantial monitoring, feedback or counseling, a null incremental result has a narrower meaning.

Exposure constrains interpretation

Delivered support is not equivalent to received support. Weak or uncertain exposure limits what can be inferred from a null behavioral result.

Maintenance may mean preventing decline

Preserved activity relative to a declining comparator is kept distinct from continued gain.

Behaviour and downstream outcomes can diverge

Changes in walking or activity do not automatically imply corresponding changes in fitness, weight or other physiological outcomes.

External Validation Gate

What remains open

v0.2 is publicly browsable but not frozen. Its internal development is complete; external methodological validation is still pending.

The current expert check asks whether the operational mapping of PA006, PA010, PA012 and PA013 onto implementation/exposure versus incremental behavioral-effect failure is defensible, and whether preserved behavior is a useful descriptive maintenance outcome category.

Method Boundary

What this map does and does not claim

This is a selected-study structural map for comparison, correction and hypothesis generation. It is not a systematic review, meta-analysis, causal model, risk-of-bias assessment or intervention-effectiveness ranking.

The working construct behavioral salience is used only as a bridge for hypothesis generation. It is not presented as an established validated theory.
