## Extracted evidence
- Qualitative longitudinal period: June 2021–April 2023.
- 88 participants: 62 interviews involving 66 people and four focus groups involving 22 patients/carers. Four think-aloud interviews are part of interview set, not four additional people.
- Five GP practice sites across England; approximately 60 hours of site observation; document review around 100 items.
- Data collection and analysis informed by NASSS; thematic analysis across qualitative materials.
- Service-feature availability varied by practice, patient care pathway and time; appointment booking not necessarily enabled; utility varied across clinical needs.
- Findings relate to access, implementation, use and sustainability of a patient portal; not an RCT of physical-activity behaviour change.
